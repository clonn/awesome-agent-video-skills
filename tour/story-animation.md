# Make a story or animation

You want an animated tale, short drama or cinematic scene.

## Three picks

1. **[gnipbao/story-to-handdrawn-video](../skills/gnipbao__story-to-handdrawn-video.md)** (Claude Skill, 2.1k stars, MIT): Turns Chinese stories or ordered images into 3:4 portrait hand-drawn animations rendered with Remotion; the README is mostly Chinese.
2. **[lemomo-ai/lemo-opuscar](../skills/lemomo-ai__lemo-opuscar.md)** (AI Tool, 585 stars, MIT): A library of film-style prompts, each backed by a short film made entirely in code, for an agent to direct your story.
3. **[Mr-funny/hbg-life-simulation](https://github.com/Mr-funny/hbg-life-simulation) ([catalog](../catalog/story-and-animation.md))** (Claude Skill, 140 stars, MIT): Turns long Chinese life-story scripts into narrated comic-style videos with a consistent character (README, in Chinese).

## Before you start

- Two of these three READMEs are written in Chinese; the cards summarize what matters.
- Install methods: [install guide](../docs/install.md). Choosing a base: [choosing a stack](../docs/choosing-a-stack.md).
