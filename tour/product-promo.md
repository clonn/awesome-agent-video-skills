# Make a product promo or demo

You want a launch film, ad or feature demo for a product.

## Three picks

1. **[Vincentwei1021/video-shotcraft](../skills/Vincentwei1021__video-shotcraft.md)** (Claude Skill, 9.9k stars, Apache-2.0): Shot recipe cards plus a Remotion template for cinematic product films; the license is Apache-2.0.
2. **[op7418/guizang-product-video-skill](../skills/op7418__guizang-product-video-skill.md)** (Claude Skill, 598 stars, AGPL-3.0): Builds software-update promo videos in code from real product components, with original music and sound effects.
3. **[gorkem-bwl/onboarding-video-generator](https://github.com/gorkem-bwl/onboarding-video-generator) ([catalog](../catalog/product-promos-and-demos.md))** (Claude Skill, 31 stars, MIT): Produces short, silent, looping feature videos in Remotion sized for a two-column web page (README default: 1280x960, about 10-15 seconds).
