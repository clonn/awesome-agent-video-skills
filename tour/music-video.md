# Make a music video

You want a video for a song, made in code.

## Three picks

1. **[JohnHeibel/PDoomVideo](../skills/JohnHeibel__PDoomVideo.md)** (AI Tool, 1.5k stars, —): Complete source of a p5.js music video generated by Claude; a showcase to study, with no license file.
2. **[ledbetterljoshua/functional-emotions-video](../skills/ledbetterljoshua__functional-emotions-video.md)** (AI Tool, 57 stars, MIT): Source of a painted music video made in Claude Code with a custom brush renderer; a showcase to study.
3. **[heygen-com/hyperframes](../skills/heygen-com__hyperframes.md)** (Agent Tool, 54.1k stars, Apache-2.0): Our suggestion, not a music-video tool: this category has only two entries, so a general base that renders HTML animation to MP4 is the way to start your own.

## Before you start

- Both music entries are showcases (finished projects), not reusable tools.
- Install methods: [install guide](../docs/install.md). Choosing a base: [choosing a stack](../docs/choosing-a-stack.md).
